Your Education Journey Starts Here

Bulgaria offers a range of higher education options, including programmes in medicine, engineering, business, information technology and other subjects. Tuition fees, admission criteria and teaching languages vary by university and programme.

Affordable Education

Indicative annual tuition ranges from €562 to €5,360 for bachelor's programmes and from €1,945 to €5,360 for master's programmes. Always confirm the exact fee with the university.

Living Costs

Plan for approximately €500–€900 per month for accommodation, food, transport and personal expenses. Your actual budget will depend on your city and lifestyle.

English-Taught Programmes

English-language options are available in fields such as medicine, engineering, business and IT. Check each university's current programme catalogue and entry requirements.

European Education Framework

Bulgaria participates in the Bologna Process and uses the European Credit Transfer and Accumulation System (ECTS). Recognition of a specific qualification depends on the programme and the relevant authority or employer.

Student Visa and Residence Requirements

Requirements depend on your nationality, length of study and individual circumstances.

EU/EEA Students

EU/EEA students generally do not need a student visa. Registration or other residence formalities may still apply depending on the length and circumstances of the stay.

Non-EU Students

Students from outside the EU generally need university admission and may need a long-stay Visa D followed by a Bulgarian residence permit. Confirm the exact procedure with the Bulgarian embassy or consulate responsible for your place of residence.

After Arrival

Address registration and other formalities may be required shortly after arrival. The European Commission's Bulgaria profile states that the address must be registered within five days. Confirm how this applies to your accommodation and individual situation before travelling.

Official reference: European Commission — Visa requirements and application process

Can Students Work While Studying?

EU students generally have the right to work in Bulgaria without a work permit, subject to the rules that apply to employment. Eligible non-EU students enrolled in full-time higher education may generally work up to 20 hours per week during the academic year and during official university holidays, subject to applicable legal and registration requirements.

Do not rely on part-time work as your only source of funding. Check the current rules with the Bulgarian Employment Agency, Migration Directorate and your university before accepting a job.

Reference: Bulgarian Employment Agency

Typical Application Documents

Each university sets its own admissions requirements. Applicants may be asked to prepare:

  • High school diploma or previous qualification
  • Academic transcript or record of grades
  • Proof of English or Bulgarian language proficiency, if required
  • Medical certificate, where required
  • Copy of a valid passport
  • Completed university application form
  • Certified translations, legalisation or recognition documents, if required

Some programmes, especially medicine and other competitive courses, may require entrance examinations or additional documents. Confirm the exact checklist and application deadline with the university before submitting anything.
