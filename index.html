A Clearer Starting Point for Bulgaria

Bulgaria is a member of the European Union and the Schengen Area. The appropriate immigration route depends on your nationality, circumstances, and the rules that apply to your case.

Our website provides general information to help you understand topics to explore before proceeding.

Important information

A Bulgarian residence permit does not automatically grant the right to live or work in every European country. Short visits within the Schengen Area are subject to applicable conditions. Approval of any application is determined by the competent authorities.
