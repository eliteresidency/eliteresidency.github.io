Prepare

Basic document checklist

  • Valid passport or travel document
  • Appropriate visa or residence application
  • Proof of accommodation, where required
  • Proof of sufficient funds, where required
  • Health insurance, where required
  • Employment, study or family documents, depending on route
  • Civil-status documents and certified translations where required
Important: Requirements differ by country, immigration route and personal circumstances. Check the current official government or embassy requirements before applying.
