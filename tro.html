What a TRO is

A Bulgarian representation structure for a foreign company.

According to the Bulgarian Chamber of Commerce and Industry (BCCI), a Trade Representation Office of a foreign entity is a non-juridical entity and is not entitled to carry out economic activities. Registration with BCCI is compulsory for these offices.

Foreign company

The application concerns an existing foreign legal person/commercial company and requires official evidence of its current legal status.

Company history & finances

Current BCCI requirements include tax-compliance evidence and turnover documentation for the relevant preceding years.

Representatives

Current BCCI guidance states that a maximum of two representatives can obtain a residence permit through this route, subject to the legal requirements.

Key current requirements

Company status

Official document confirming the current legal status of the foreign company, generally issued within the period specified by BCCI.

Tax compliance

Evidence concerning the absence of tax liabilities for the required preceding period.

Turnover

Current BCCI guidance specifies annual turnover of at least BGN 100,000 (or equivalent) for each of the preceding two years for initial registration.

Office in Bulgaria

Evidence of an office for the representation entity, such as an appropriate rental or property document meeting BCCI requirements.

Programme & powers

A programme of planned activities and the required power of attorney and representative signature documentation.

Important: Requirements are based on current BCCI information and Bulgarian law. We recommend confirming the exact document list before filing because rules can change.
