Bulgarian Business & Immigration Information

Trade Representative Office (TRO)

Explore how a foreign company's trade representative office in Bulgaria may relate to a Bulgarian residence application. Understand the process before taking your next step.

Explore the Process

What Is a Trade Representative Office?

A Trade Representative Office, often called a TRO, provides a foreign company with a representative presence in Bulgaria. Depending on the company's circumstances and the applicable rules, establishing a TRO may be relevant to a representative's Bulgarian immigration application.

Business registration and immigration approval are separate procedures. Establishing a TRO does not, by itself, guarantee a visa or a residence permit.

Who May Consider This Route?

This route may be worth exploring for representatives of established foreign companies who want to understand the legal options for living in Bulgaria.

Eligibility depends on the foreign company, its business background, the proposed representative arrangements and the current Bulgarian legal requirements. An individual assessment is important before making commitments.

How Does the Process Generally Work?

1. Review the foreign company

Review the company's registration, history, business activities and available supporting documents.

2. Check the applicable requirements

Confirm the current rules for establishing a Bulgarian trade representative office and appointing representatives.

3. Prepare the documentation

Depending on the case, company records, representative details, identity documents and certified or translated documents may be required.

4. Complete the relevant registration steps

Prepare and submit the necessary documentation to the appropriate Bulgarian bodies.

5. Assess the immigration application

Determine whether the representative meets the requirements for a Bulgarian long-stay visa and any subsequent residence application. The applicable visa and residence conditions must be checked separately.

6. Follow the official procedure

Submit the required applications and supporting evidence, attend appointments when requested and respond to any additional document requests.

Please note: Documents, fees, eligibility criteria and processing times vary by case and may change. Confirm the current requirements before applying.

Financial Planning and Eligibility

Before proceeding, applicants should establish which legal route applies to their circumstances and what financial, company-related and personal evidence it requires.

Do not assume that a particular investment amount or financial commitment applies to every TRO case. Any proposed amount, including €152,000 per year for two years, must be verified against the specific legal basis before being treated as an official requirement.

Bulgaria and the Rest of Europe

Bulgaria is a member of the European Union and the Schengen Area. A valid Bulgarian residence document may permit eligible short visits to other Schengen countries, subject to the applicable conditions.

Bulgarian residence does not automatically grant the right to live or work in every EU country. Moving to another country may require a separate permit or other authorisation under that country's rules.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does TRO registration guarantee a residence permit?

No. Registration and immigration approval are separate. The applicant must satisfy the relevant immigration rules.

Can every foreign company establish a TRO for this purpose?

Not necessarily. The applicable requirements and the circumstances of the company and representative must be assessed before proceeding.

How long does the process take?

There is no single timeline for every applicant. Document preparation, registration, visa processing and residence procedures may each take different amounts of time.

Which documents will I need?

Requirements depend on the route and individual case. Obtain an up-to-date checklist before ordering translations, certifications or legalisations.

Can Bulgarian residence let me work anywhere in Europe?

No. Bulgarian residence does not automatically give unrestricted residence or employment rights in all EU countries.
