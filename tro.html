Trade Representative Office in Bulgaria

Discuss Your Case

What is a Bulgarian TRO?

A Trade Representative Office (TRO) is registered in Bulgaria through the Bulgarian Chamber of Commerce and Industry (BCCI). It is a representative structure for a foreign company, not a separate Bulgarian commercial company.

Important: A TRO is not entitled to conduct independent economic or commercial activity in Bulgaria. Registration does not guarantee a residence permit. The relevant authorities assess each application under the applicable rules.

Company turnover: our screening threshold

Before accepting a TRO-related case, Elite Residency uses the following minimum client-screening threshold for the foreign company:

EUR 152,000 per year for each of the preceding two years

This means at least EUR 152,000 in Year 1 and EUR 152,000 in Year 2.

This is Elite Residency's own screening threshold. It is not presented as the statutory Bulgarian minimum. Official eligibility requirements and evidence must be verified for each case before proceeding.

Who may be eligible?

Eligibility depends on the foreign company, its documentation, the proposed representative and the current Bulgarian rules. Before proceeding, relevant factors may include:

  • The foreign company's registration and business history.
  • Evidence of the company's activity and financial position.
  • Required corporate and representative documents.
  • Compliance with current BCCI and Bulgarian residence requirements.
  • Any applicable limits on the number of representatives who may qualify through this route.

These are general considerations, not a complete legal checklist or a promise of approval.
