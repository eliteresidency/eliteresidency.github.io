# Bulgarian Trade Representative Office (TRO) ## Explore a business-based route to Bulgarian residence A Trade Representative Office (TRO) is a way for an established foreign company to establish a representative presence in Bulgaria. For eligible applicants, this route may form part of an application for a Bulgarian long-stay visa and residence permit. The process involves separate business-registration and immigration requirements. Registering an office alone does not guarantee immigration approval. ## Who may consider this route? This option may be relevant to business owners or representatives of an established foreign company who want to explore a legal basis for living in Bulgaria. Eligibility depends on the foreign company, its business history, the proposed representative arrangements and the current Bulgarian legal requirements. Each case should be assessed individually. ## How the process generally works 1. **Review the foreign company** — assess its history, activities and supporting records. 2. **Check eligibility** — confirm the current requirements for establishing a Bulgarian trade representative office and naming representatives. 3. **Prepare the documents** — gather the company records and any translations, certifications or legalisations required for the case. 4. **Complete the registration process** — arrange the necessary filings with the relevant Bulgarian bodies. 5. **Review the immigration route** — determine whether the applicant qualifies to apply for a Bulgarian long-stay visa and, subsequently, a residence permit. 6. **Follow the official procedure** — submit the required applications and attend any appointments requested by the authorities. The exact steps, documents, fees and processing times depend on the applicant’s circumstances and the rules in force when applying. ## Bulgaria and the rest of Europe Bulgaria is part of the European Union and the Schengen Area. A valid Bulgarian residence document may allow eligible short visits to other Schengen countries under the applicable rules. However, Bulgarian residence does **not** automatically give someone the right to live or work in every EU country. Other countries may require a separate visa, permit or application. ## Frequently asked questions **Does registering a TRO guarantee residence?** No. Registration and immigration approval are separate matters. Applicants must satisfy the applicable immigration requirements. **Can any foreign company use this route?** Not necessarily. The company and the proposed representatives must meet the requirements in force. The company’s history and genuine business activity may be relevant. **How long does the process take?** There is no single timeline that applies to every case. Registration, document preparation, consular processing and residence procedures can have different timeframes. **What documents will I need?** Requirements vary by case. Company records, proof of business activity, representative details, identity documents and properly prepared supporting documents may be needed. Confirm the current checklist before preparing an application. **Can I work anywhere in Europe after receiving Bulgarian residence?** No. Bulgarian residence does not automatically confer unrestricted residence or employment rights across the EU. ## Start with an individual assessment Contact Elite Residency to discuss your circumstances and identify the questions that should be checked before you begin.
