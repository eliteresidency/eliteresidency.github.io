Residence in Bulgaria

Bulgaria offers different residence pathways for eligible foreign nationals. The appropriate option depends on your nationality, personal circumstances, purpose of stay and the immigration rules that apply to your situation.

Understanding the relevant route before preparing documents can help you plan the process more effectively. Requirements should always be checked against current official guidance.

Explore Possible Residence Routes

Trade Representative Office (TRO)

For some representatives of established foreign companies, a Bulgarian trade representative office may be relevant to an immigration application. Company registration and residence approval are separate procedures, and eligibility must be assessed individually.

Learn more about the TRO route →

Long-stay visa and residence procedures

Some residence routes require a long-stay visa before a residence application can be made in Bulgaria. The applicable procedure depends on the legal basis for the application.

Other residence options

Other routes may be available depending on factors such as family circumstances, employment, study, business activities or other legally recognised grounds. Not every route is available to every applicant.

How to Prepare

1. Identify the applicable route

Clarify the purpose of your stay and determine which legal basis may apply to your circumstances.

2. Check the current requirements

Confirm eligibility, document requirements, translations, certifications and any applicable financial evidence.

3. Prepare your documents

Collect the documents required for your specific route and check that they meet the relevant format and validity rules.

4. Submit the appropriate application

Follow the instructions of the competent authorities and attend any required appointments.

Important: This is a general overview, not a complete application checklist. Requirements and procedures may change.

Bulgarian Residence and Europe

Bulgaria is a member of the European Union and the Schengen Area. A valid Bulgarian residence document may permit eligible short visits to other Schengen countries under the applicable rules.

However, Bulgarian residence does not automatically grant the right to live or work in every EU country. Separate permission may be required to settle or work elsewhere.
