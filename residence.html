Residence in Bulgaria

Bulgaria is a member of the European Union and offers various residence routes for eligible foreign nationals. The right option depends on your nationality, circumstances, and the immigration rules applicable to your situation.

Elite Residency provides information and assistance to help applicants understand the process and prepare the relevant documentation.

Trade Representative Office (TRO)

A foreign company may be able to register a Trade Representative Office in Bulgaria. Under applicable conditions, this may be relevant to a residence application for a foreign representative.

Registering an office does not automatically guarantee a residence permit. Applicants must meet the applicable legal requirements.

Financial Planning

Planning figure: €152,000 per year for two years.

This figure is included for service-planning purposes only. It is not represented as an official government minimum or statutory immigration requirement. Please confirm the purpose and applicable requirements before making any financial commitment.

Documents and Application Process

Depending on your residence route, the process may involve:

  • Checking eligibility for the relevant residence category.
  • Preparing company and representative-office documents, where applicable.
  • Obtaining the appropriate visa, if required.
  • Submitting an application to the competent authorities.
  • Providing additional documents requested by the authorities.

Requirements vary by individual circumstances. Check the current rules with the Bulgarian authorities or a qualified immigration professional.

Access to Europe

Bulgaria is a member of the European Union and the Schengen Area. A valid Bulgarian residence permit may allow short visits to other Schengen countries, subject to applicable conditions.

Bulgarian residence does not automatically grant the right to live or work in every European country. Separate national requirements may apply.
