Residence in Bulgaria

Bulgaria can be a base for eligible non-EU nationals who wish to explore residence options and establish business connections in Europe. The appropriate route depends on your nationality, circumstances, and the applicable Bulgarian immigration requirements.

Elite Residency provides information and assistance with understanding the process, preparing documentation, and identifying the relevant authorities and requirements.

Trade Representative Office (TRO)

A foreign company may be able to establish a trade representative office in Bulgaria. In certain circumstances, this may be relevant to a residence application by a foreign representative.

Registration of a representative office does not automatically guarantee a residence permit. Eligibility and supporting documents must be assessed under the rules in force.

Financial Planning

This is a planning figure requested for the service information. It is not presented as an official Bulgarian government minimum or a statutory immigration requirement. Please confirm the amount, its purpose, and any applicable legal requirements before making a financial commitment.

Residence Documents and Application Process

Depending on your situation, the process may involve:

  • Checking eligibility for the relevant residence route.
  • Preparing company and representative-office documents, where applicable.
  • Obtaining the appropriate visa, if required.
  • Submitting the residence application to the competent authorities.
  • Providing any additional documents requested by the authorities.

Requirements can vary by nationality and individual circumstances. Always check the current rules with the Bulgarian authorities or a qualified immigration professional.

Access to Europe

Bulgaria is a member of the European Union. However, Bulgarian residence permission does not grant an automatic right to live or work in every European country.

Travel within the Schengen Area is subject to the applicable entry rules, permitted duration of stay, and your individual immigration status. Living or working in another country generally requires meeting that country's rules.
