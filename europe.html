Bulgaria and Europe

Understand how Bulgarian residence relates to travel in Europe, and why residence and work rights differ between countries.

Explore the Guide

Why Consider Bulgaria?

Bulgaria is a member of the European Union and the Schengen Area. Foreign nationals who qualify under Bulgarian law may be able to apply for a suitable visa or residence status, depending on their individual circumstances.

The correct route depends on the applicant's purpose of stay, nationality, supporting evidence and the legal requirements in force. There is no single residence option suitable for everyone.

Bulgarian Residence and Schengen Travel

A valid Bulgarian residence permit or long-stay visa may allow eligible short visits to other Schengen countries, subject to the applicable rules, travel-document requirements and permitted duration of stay.

Permission to make a short visit is different from permission to move permanently, take employment or establish a business in another country.

Important: Check the current entry conditions and permitted length of stay before travelling. Requirements may depend on your nationality and the document you hold.

Does Bulgarian Residence Give You the Right to Live Anywhere in Europe?

No. A Bulgarian residence permit does not automatically grant the right to live or work in every EU member state.

If you plan to relocate, work or operate a business in another European country, you may need to meet that country's separate immigration, employment or business requirements.

Schengen travel rules should not be confused with national residence and work-permit rules.

Explore the Bulgarian TRO Route

A Trade Representative Office (TRO) may be relevant for certain representatives of established foreign companies considering a Bulgarian immigration route. The company's circumstances, the representative's eligibility and the current legal requirements must be assessed individually.

Registering a TRO does not automatically guarantee a visa or residence permit.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Bulgaria in the European Union?

Yes. Bulgaria is a member of the European Union.

Is Bulgaria in the Schengen Area?

Yes. Bulgaria is part of the Schengen Area.

Can I visit other Schengen countries with Bulgarian residence?

Eligible travellers may be able to make short visits under Schengen rules. The conditions depend on the document held and the applicable rules.

Can I work in another EU country with Bulgarian residence?

Not automatically. You must check the immigration and employment requirements of the country where you want to work.

Does a TRO guarantee Bulgarian residence?

No. Registration and immigration approval are separate, and the applicant must meet the applicable legal requirements.
