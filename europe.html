Bulgaria and Europe

Bulgaria is a member of the European Union and part of the Schengen Area. For eligible applicants, Bulgarian residence options may provide a starting point for building business connections and exploring opportunities in Europe.

The residence route available to you depends on your nationality, personal circumstances, and the applicable immigration rules.

Travel Within the Schengen Area

A valid Bulgarian residence permit may allow its holder to travel to other Schengen countries for short stays, subject to the applicable conditions, generally up to 90 days in any 180-day period across the other Schengen countries.

Important:

A Bulgarian residence permit does not automatically give you the right to live or work in another European country. Entry conditions, passport requirements, and permitted stays must be checked for your individual situation.

Business Opportunities in Europe

Bulgaria can be a base for exploring business relationships with companies in other European markets.

  • Explore potential business partnerships.
  • Learn about European markets and business environments.
  • Understand the requirements for establishing business activities.
  • Seek professional advice about cross-border business operations.

Business registration, taxation, licensing, and employment requirements vary between countries.

Trade Representative Office in Bulgaria

A Trade Representative Office (TRO) may be relevant for certain foreign companies seeking a business presence in Bulgaria. In qualifying cases, the foreign company's representative may be eligible to apply for residence under the applicable rules.

Establishing an office does not itself guarantee a residence permit, EU citizenship, or unrestricted access to Europe's labour markets.
